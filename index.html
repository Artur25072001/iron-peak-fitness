Who We Are

A GYM BUILT ON
GRIT & COMMUNITY

Iron Peak Fitness opened its doors in 2018 with one mission: strip away the noise and deliver results-driven training for people who show up ready to work. We don't do glossy sales pitches. We do heavy lifts, high-intensity conditioning, and a community that pushes each other past every plateau.

Whether you're stepping into a gym for the first time or you've been chasing PRs for a decade, you belong here. Our coaches meet you where you are — then they push you further.

No mirrors. No egos. Just progress.
